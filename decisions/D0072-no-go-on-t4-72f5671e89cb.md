# NO GO on `t4_72f5671e89cb`

```
O=S1(=O)CC[C@@H](N(Cc2cnoc2)C2CC(Br)=NO2)C1
C11H14BrN3O4S · MW 364.2 · QED 0.796 · SAscore 4.50 · BDHI warhead on sulfopin's sulfolane
```

**Do not send this molecule for synthesis.** The full workup is in
`docs/elevation_example.md` §8; this record states the decision and its limits.

## The decision

Four things together, no one of which is sufficient alone:

1. **Its rank came from metrics that predict nothing.** D0071 established, on a
   pre-registered cohort, that neither enrichment nor consensus predicts whether
   a docked pose survives physics. This molecule was ranked first by both. Its
   position on the list is therefore not evidence.

2. **It is the worst molecule in the cohort on the readout that does work.**
   Tier-1 warhead displacement 0.540 nm, **rank 37 of 37**, against a
   crystallographic median of 0.102 nm — and that readout separates known Cys113
   binders from generated candidates at p = 0.007. Two independently built
   systems agree: the 100 ns run has the warhead 0.620 nm out by the start of
   production.

3. **The near-attack conformation is visited, not inhabited.** "10 of 10 poses at
   3.04 Å" is true of the docking output and misleading about the molecule: 7.5%
   occupancy over the bound phase, concentrated in the 14 ns immediately before
   it left.

4. **Its warhead class has no validation at all.** Zero BDHI among the 17
   crystallographic Cys113 structures. Chloroacetamide has AUC 0.908; BDHI has
   never been tested against a single positive. This was flagged when the
   molecule was elevated and nothing since has closed it.

## What is explicitly NOT the reason

Recorded so this no-go is not later remembered as stronger than it was.

- **Dissociating at 54 ns is not a failure.** A covalent inhibitor does not need
  its warhead parked on the sulfur — it needs to reach the window, and the bond
  is permanent once formed. 54 ns is a long non-covalent residence for a 364 Da
  fragment, and @tt8804 is right that drift alone is not disqualifying.
- **7.5% occupancy is not disqualifying either.** Across the rebinding events
  that occur at working concentration, reaching the window 7.5% of the time is
  ample on chemical timescales.
- **Tier 2 does not single it out.** It sits 3rd–4th of 8 within group A. This
  decision rests on tier 1 and on the missing class validation.
- **Med chem does not object.** QED 0.796 at 364 Da is genuinely drug-like and
  SAscore 4.50 is makeable. Nothing here is a developability veto.

## What would reverse it

- **A crystallographic positive run through tier 3.** We have no 100 ns baseline
  on this receptor, so we cannot say whether 54 ns is excellent or unremarkable.
  This is the cheapest missing control and it cuts both ways.
- **Any BDHI positive against Cys113.** One structure, or a measured IC50 on a
  BDHI in this series, would move the class from unvalidated to validated and
  change what the geometric criterion is worth here.
- **A ranking metric that survives its own validation.** Deferred to 2.1.0.

## Consequence

The T_4 shortlist is not resupplied from the enrichment ordering. The next
shortlist is built in **2.1.0** on the validated warhead class plus a metric that
has passed a pre-registered stability test — not on the ordering this molecule
came from.
