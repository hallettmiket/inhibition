# The engagement metric is not reproducible

## What was measured

The 8 ns probe re-ran the top six modes and all six collapsed. Two explanations
were live and they have very different consequences:

* **A** -- the metric is noisy, the campaign's top is regression to the mean,
  and the ranking's head is largely luck;
* **B** -- the two code paths differ (the campaign runs `--stop-after npt` then
  `--reuse-equilibration`; the probe ran straight through), and the drop is an
  artefact of that.

**The control was run through the EXACT campaign path** -- `--abort-above-a 6.0`,
two-phase equilibration, 1201 ps to force a fresh work-root -- and it also came
back low. It is **A**.

| mode | campaign | re-run | 8 ns probe | spread |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `t4_2aa3875168b4_m28` | 37.2% | **0.0%** | **0.0%** | 37.2 pts |
| `t4_fbb3cd73e1e2_m142` | 38.8% | 1.7% | 0.2% | 37.2 |
| `t4_bcfa4048fd32_m161` | 33.1% | 1.7% | 2.7% | 31.4 |
| `t4_2ae730ebea05_m70` | 33.1% | 28.9% | 6.7% | 26.4 |
| `t4_2bd5ba0aa666_m187` | 33.9% | 18.2% | 15.0% | 18.9 |
| `t4_91820ab38c82_m145` | 31.4% | 15.0% | | 16.4 |
| `t4_f53c3424077a_m40` | 31.4% | 23.3% | | 8.1 |
| `t4_054b839bffdd_m143` | 30.6% | 23.3% | | 7.2 |
| `t4_15e47a29ce30_m148` | 31.4% | 30.0% | | **1.4** |

**Median spread 18.9 percentage points.** The run-to-run spread on a single mode
exceeds the entire population's median engagement (2.5%).

## Why the seeds do not save it

`gen-seed` and `ld-seed` are set per molecule and were verified **identical**
between the campaign run and the re-run (1925434818 for `t4_2bd5ba0aa666`).
Seeding fixes the initial velocities; it does not make a GPU trajectory
deterministic -- floating-point reduction order is not fixed -- and MD is
chaotic, so two runs from the same state diverge within picoseconds. Over
1.2 ns, occupancy of a narrow geometric window (2.8-3.5 A) is then a
small-sample statistic on a rapidly decorrelating quantity.

Nothing is broken. The measurement is doing exactly what it was written to do;
it is being asked a question one 1.2 ns replicate cannot answer.

## What this invalidates

* **The top of the sweep ranking.** A mode reading 33% may be a 5% mode that
  drew well. The nine tested were selected as the top of 683 and every one
  regressed -- which is what selection on a noisy measure produces.
* **Any elevation decision resting on a single 1.2 ns engagement figure.**
  The 60% gate was never reached, so nothing was elevated on it; that is now
  fortunate rather than disappointing.

## What it does NOT invalidate

* **The give-up triage.** `start_dist_a` and the 6 A abort separate poses that
  have left from poses that have not, and that is a coarse call on a quantity
  that does not need 1.2 ns of averaging.
* **The survival readout from the adaptive runs.** How long a mode lasts before
  leaving is a coarser, later-integrating quantity than window occupancy, and it
  is measured over up to 10 ns rather than 1.2. Its own reproducibility has NOT
  been measured and must not be assumed -- that is the next control.
* **PoseBusters validity, mode assignment, or anything upstream of the MD.**

## Why it was not caught earlier

Because nothing had ever been run twice. The project's own catalogue names this
route -- *"ran the pipeline on something whose answer was already written
down"* -- and D0044 recorded implicit + explicit MD residence as not
reproducible on this same system. The sweep was built as a triage and its
single-replicate output was then read as a ranking, which is a different claim.

Every honest number here cost one re-run of work already on disk.

## What follows

1. **Report engagement with a spread, or not at all.** A single-replicate figure
   should not be printed as a rank-ordering key.
2. **Measure the variance properly** on a RANDOM sample of modes, not the top --
   the nine here are a selected set and their spread mixes noise with regression
   to the mean.
3. **Prefer readouts that integrate longer.** The adaptive change (2026-09-03)
   already runs survivors to 10 ns, giving ~8x the independent samples per
   occupancy estimate; whether that is enough is an empirical question.
4. **Replicates are the honest fix** and cost 3x. Whether the choreography can
   afford them for 4,295 modes, or should sweep fewer modes with replicates, is
   a decision for the PI.
